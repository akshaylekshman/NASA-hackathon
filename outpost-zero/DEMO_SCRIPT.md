# Two-minute demo script

**0:00–0:10 — Hook:** “It is turn six. The lunar night has started, the panels are quiet, and our outpost is running on stored energy.”

**0:10–0:25 — Problem:** “Four crew members need air, water, food, and power. Each build spends time and materials, so we cannot solve every problem at once.”

**0:25–1:25 — Gameplay:** “Here is the mission briefing and seed. I’ll make a prediction about the next turn, choose a system to build, and resolve two Earth days. The dashboard shows the crew and supplies. The Flow Map exposes recorded links between sunlight, panels, power, and life support. In the debrief we can follow a meter change back through the causes the simulation recorded.”

**1:25–1:45 — Learning:** “Rookie, Cadet, and Commander change the support level. The Codex introduces space concepts, and a shared seed lets a class compare decisions under the same schedule.”

**1:45–1:55 — Data and credits:** “This runs offline with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Resource quantities are simplified game units, not engineering specifications.”

**1:55–2:00 — Close:** “Could your crew make it through the lunar night?”
